About the Show

A podcast series amplifying women's voices in the supply chain and logistics industry.

Ladies, Leadership & Logistics (LLL) brings real conversations with the women leading supply chains, ports, and operations across the globe. 🎙️ Through candid interviews with leaders in the field, we share insights, inspiration, and guidance for the next generation of professionals. 🚀

Every episode is a stop on the same route — women navigating an industry built on movement, and making sure their voices move with it.

Behind the Mic

The Founder

Aayushi Desai

Founder & Host- Ladies, Leadership & Logistics

Supply chain & logistics professional based in Mumbai — building a platform for women's leadership in an industry that still needs it.

Connect on LinkedIn
“India's logistics cost is 13–14% of GDP. In Germany, it's under 9%. That gap isn't just a statistic — it's the problem I'm here to work on.”

I'm a supply chain and logistics professional based in Mumbai, with an MBA in Global Logistics & Port Management from KU Leuven, Belgium. My work sits at the intersection of strategy, operations, and India's fast-evolving logistics landscape. My experience spans three countries and three kinds of work:

  • EDR Antwerp Shipyard (MSC partner) — operational analysis, mapping logistics structures, designing process flows, and identifying inefficiencies across import-export operations.
  • Foronex, Belgium — consulting engagement auditing logistics operations, building cost transparency frameworks, and improving supplier coordination.
  • Semiconductor chemical logistics — research and compliance work across 800+ companies.

I currently work in logistics and operations at ASL Logistics, where I'm building ground-level understanding of how Indian supply chains actually function — not just how they're supposed to.

Alongside this, I founded and host Ladies, Leadership & Logistics, a podcast series featuring 50+ executives and leaders in global supply chain, from Coca-Cola, Lupin, Black Box, and beyond. We've generated 66K+ impressions and 8.8K+ clicks, and the show has become a small but real platform for women's leadership in a sector that still needs it.

I'm looking to build a career in supply chain strategy and consulting — specifically helping Indian companies strengthen their logistics infrastructure, reduce costs, and navigate the complexity of scaling operations. If you work in logistics, supply chain, or operations, and especially if you're a woman doing interesting work in this space, I'd genuinely like to connect.
